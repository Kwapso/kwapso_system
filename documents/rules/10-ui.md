# UI shell & interfaces

Lean cross-index for the `lean_foundation` score. The laws below prove most of the structural,
mechanically-checkable shape of the UI (tab strips, shared form shells, spacing, colour tokens,
kit-only composition); criterion-review is deliberately narrower, judging only the arrangement
and feel calls that sit *above* what these laws already enforce. See
`~/.claude/skills/criterion-review/criteria/10-ui.md` for the full rubric. Source of truth for
every law remains RULES.md + `shared/rules/registry.ts`. This is expected to be the largest of
the twelve files — most of the client's UI rulings became laws here.

### Tab strips, forms & panels

- **R2** — Every record-detail screen draws its tab strip through the shared TabsView, and its history is reachable from the footer's Latest activity rail. (check: `record-detail-tabs`, enforced)
- **R3** — Collection tab strips use the shared TabsView component; no hand-rolled button toggles standing in for tabs. (check: `no-handrolled-toggles`, enforced)
- **R4** — Every form or dialog renders through the shared FormShell layout (title, separator, fields, separator, action). (check: `forms-use-formshell`, enforced)
- **R8** — Every tab that reveals a collection carries that collection's exact count, derived from the block it opens rather than hand-typed. (check: `tab-counts-derived`, enforced)
- **R51** — A collapsing side panel collapses in place; it is never unmounted mid-animation and never stays reachable once closed. (check: `aside-collapse`, enforced)
- **R59** — A form, editor or picker slides in as a Sheet; only a yes/no warning is a centred dialog overlay. (check: `forms-are-not-overlays`, enforced)
- **R61** — A module gets a settings gear only if it has something to set, and every module's settings are also reachable once from one central Modules list. (check: `module-settings-two-doors`, enforced)
- **R64** — Every section that used to live on the team area's tab strip still has a door somewhere, and that screen still carries its acts. (check: `sections-have-a-door`, enforced)
- **R70** — Every automatic app behaviour is listed on its module's settings page with a stated on/off switch, or a named reason it can't be switched. (check: `automations-are-visible`, enforced)
- **R72** — No subtitle renders under a heading unless the design explicitly calls for one. (check: `no-default-subtitles`, enforced)
- **R77** — Every screen's own tab strip stays pinned to the top while the screen scrolls, not only a collection's. (check: `tab-strips-pin`, enforced)
- **R81** — A form shows only the label and the field, no helper sentence or explanation underneath, by default. (check: `form-carries-no-hints`, enforced)
- **R105** — Inside a sheet, every fact label (Assigned to, Details, Deadline…) shares one small-caps eyebrow style, and sheet sections paint no background. (check: `sheet-fact-labels`, enforced)

### Toolbars, search & collections

- **R16** — Every collection screen shows its exact count exactly once, through one shared count seam, never duplicated between a tab badge and a heading. (check: `counted-collections`, enforced)
- **R48** — Every collection/data-view screen shows a search box by default, unless a named, reasoned exemption says otherwise. (check: `toolbar-shows-search`, enforced)
- **R49** — The gap between a toolbar and the content below it is one shared value the toolbar pays itself; no screen adds its own extra margin. (check: `toolbar-content-gap`, enforced)
- **R50** — A toolbar draws nothing at all, not even the create button, while its collection is empty; emptiness is checked before any slot. (check: `empty-toolbar`, enforced)
- **R53** — The toolbar's five slots (search, filters, sort, view, actions) are fixed and built from structured configs, never hand-placed nodes; every call site supplies a sort or names why not. (check: `toolbar-slot-set`, enforced)
- **R62** — A collection's "no rows exist yet" and "filters found nothing" states share one register; only the add button differs between them. (check: `one-zero-register`, enforced)
- **R63** — A collection's toolbar stays pinned to the top of the screen while its rows scroll underneath it, everywhere. (check: `pinned-toolbar`, enforced)
- **R74** — Opening Import always opens a new solo tab; it never redirects the tab you were already working in. (check: `import-opens-a-tab`, enforced)
- **R75** — Every list of pickable options (a filter, a Select, a picker) sorts A to Z in the viewer's own language. (check: `alphabetical-options`, enforced)
- **R78** — Calendar, week, agenda and timeline views show no sort control, since the time axis is already the order. (check: `no-sort-in-calendar-views`, enforced)
- **R80** — Rows render as the plain column list Tickets already uses, never as a bordered/banded table. (check: `rows-are-a-list`, enforced)
- **R82** — A table row shows at most six columns; a seventh field moves elsewhere rather than being squeezed onto the end. (check: `table-column-budget`, enforced)
- **R83** — A tab strip and the toolbar it labels share one gapless column; the toolbar's lead gap is paid once, never doubled by a card or wrapper. (check: `toolbar-lead-gap`, enforced)
- **R88** — When a section is empty, its own empty-state "Add the first" is the only way to add, and the section's header draws nothing beside it. (check: `empty-state-single-door`, enforced)
- **R101** — Every toolbar's search field starts flush at the content edge on every screen; no toolbar host adds its own side inset. (check: `toolbar-search-edge`, enforced)
- **R103** — An empty or filtered-empty collection shows plain text on the page background, never a card or paper fill. (check: `empty-register-on-the-page`, enforced)

### Records, chips & faces

- **R35** — A record or dropdown value never appears without its visual: its own picture, its type's glyph, or its initial, wherever it's picked or listed. (check: `records-carry-their-face`, enforced)
- **R40** — Every field that stores a file's bytes is claimed by name and actually rendered somewhere a person can open it (an href, src or picture), never write-only. (check: `reachable-bytes`, enforced)
- **R41** — A file picked before a record exists is either sent once the record is created, or refused; it is never silently dropped by a discarded create-time result. (check: `picked-files-are-sent`, enforced)
- **R52** — Every record-detail screen uses the same title/actions split and head layout, one shared constant, never a per-screen class. (check: `record-title-treatment`, enforced)
- **R65** — On a card that stands for a record, its status/type chip sits above the title, not below it. (check: `chip-above-title`, enforced)
- **R79** — Staff is chosen from a horizontal pill row, never a dropdown, with the signed-in user preselected and no "Nobody" option. (check: `staff-pill-row`, enforced)
- **R86** — In any collection, the one coloured chip is always the record's status; every other categorical field gets an icon or plain text (task priority is the sole named exception). (check: `status-owns-the-chip`, enforced)
- **R87** — Every title-shaped field is capped at 50 characters with a live counter, the write door refuses the same ceiling, and every one-line renderer truncates with an ellipsis. (check: `title-length`, enforced)
- **R90** — A choice control over people shows the same avatar/initials the record lists already show, in both the option list and the chosen value. (check: `faces-in-choices`, enforced)
- **R92** — When a main person is picked (Raised by, Assignee, Owner…), that person is excluded from the paired secondary picker over the same pool. (check: `main-excludes-secondary`, enforced)
- **R93** — Wherever a record has its own avatar, icon or colour, that visual rides beside its text in filters, views and selects; only a chip's own avatar/logo is exempt. (check: `visual-accompanies-text`, enforced)
- **R94** — A record's chip row always orders as id, status, type, main parent, secondary parent, drawn through one shared ordering seam. (check: `chip-order`, enforced)
- **R96** — A record's id chip is always the kit's black ink fill, never a hand-rolled badge in another colour and never bare text. (check: `id-chip-is-black`, enforced)
- **R97** — A number that counts related things (tickets, stories, stakeholders) sits beside its panel's own title; it never gets its own card or stat tile. (check: `counts-beside-titles`, enforced)
- **R102** — A table column that renders a record's reference chip is as narrow as the chip itself, headed with the record's own noun, never "ID". (check: `id-column-noun`, enforced)
- **R104** — A person or group of people inside a record section renders as plain PersonCard chips under an eyebrow label, never inside their own card. (check: `people-as-chips`, enforced)
- **R108** — Every record section's own title (Assigned to, Effort, Stakeholders…) shares the same small-caps eyebrow style as a sheet's fact labels, never a separate look. (check: `section-title-one-style`, enforced)

### Layout, structure, colour & motion

- **R25** — Any screen showing a savings figure renders the fixed SAVINGS_CAPTION explaining the times are agreed estimates and the subtraction is arithmetic. (check: `savings-caption`, enforced)
- **R29** — Each front door owns exactly one page-width container; no other component sets its own page-level max-width. (check: `one-page-width`, enforced)
- **R31** — Only two corner radii exist app-wide (a rectangle's and a pill's, plus one named selection-control exception); no third radius value. (check: `two-radii`, enforced)
- **R32** — Every colour resolves through a design token; no Tailwind colour ramp and no hex literal outside a short reviewed list. (check: `closed-palette`, enforced)
- **R39** — All UI (controls, structure, glyphs, toasts) comes from the vendored design kit; no app file imports a UI package directly. (check: `kit-supplies-the-ui`, enforced)
- **R45** — Every whole-screen composition the design kit ships is either actually adopted somewhere in the app or has a reasoned exemption on file. (check: `composition-coverage`, enforced)
- **R46** — Every component and foundation the design kit ships is either actually reached (directly or through another adopted part) or has a reasoned exemption on file. (check: `component-coverage`, enforced)
- **R60** — Every image fills its box (object-cover) and is cropped to fit; it is never shrunk to fit inside its box. (check: `image-fills`, enforced)
- **R67** — A grouping section paints only a fill or an inset shadow for separation, never a stroke, and only a design token for colour, never a hex value. (check: `sections-stand-on-paper`, enforced)
- **R84** — The brand-mango button colour appears only inside a screen's own title component; every other button (toolbar, dialog, sheet, empty state) is the inverse/black style. (check: `mango-in-title-only`, enforced)
- **R85** — Every navigation rail destination is named in exactly one word, no space or hyphen. (check: `rail-labels-one-word`, enforced)
- **R89** — A record screen's dark footer band (Latest activity + Record) is the last element on the page, flush with the bottom edge, with the standard panel gap above it. (check: `footer-on-the-edge`, enforced)
- **R91** — The page scrolls once, as a whole; chat threads, the rail, the assistant pane and true overlays are the only nested-scroll exceptions. (check: `no-nested-scroll`, enforced)
- **R98** — Every button in a toolbar, page head, card header or form foot uses the kit's default height or icon size, never a smaller custom size. (check: `button-sizes`, enforced)
- **R100** — A screen head's action (a settings gear, an edit pencil) always centres vertically against the title's own line, never the whole stacked title+subtitle block. (check: `head-actions-centred`, enforced)
- **R106** — A record page fills the shared footer slot exactly once through one component; a sheet draws it as its own last stripe outside the scroller. (check: `footer-band-home`, enforced)
- **R107** — The Effort section's metric tiles span a three-column grid on the title's own edge, and the log rows below carry no fill, separated by one shared divider. (check: `effort-tiles`, enforced)
- **R109** — Every screen carries the shell's one content inset by default; no screen sets its own left/right margin. (check: `content-inset`, enforced)
