# The exam — from four weeks of meetings that actually have transcripts

Window: 13 Aug – 10 Sep 2026. Only meetings whose transcript or Gemini notes exist on
staging with **15 or more pieces** were used (71 sources; checked 10 Sep). Every row names
the source that answers it and its size, so Lane E can key the ids after the re-index.
Questions are written the way a person asks, never the way the meeting was titled.

Tags: `para` paraphrase · `exact` reference/number · `count` needs a tool · `latest`
recency · `multi` two sources joined · `person` colleague/contact · `de` German · `fence`
permission · `absent` must refuse · `gap` the meeting happened but no notes exist ·
`hijack` ordinary word that is also a name · `route` live record · `synth` many sources.

Levels: **E** easy · **M** medium (no title words) · **H** hard (joins, time) ·
**X** adversarial · **G** honest gaps · **DE** German.
